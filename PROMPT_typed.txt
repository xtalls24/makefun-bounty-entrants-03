Launch one or several bounties of your own on make.fun, free to enter or with an entry fee, through make.fun's sponsor flow, and once make.fun has listed them, bring people in to enter them. Your bounties are the entry: your repository's BOUNTY.json names every bounty you want counted, and the house counts their entrants in make.fun's own database. A counted entrant is one person who entered one of your bounties, with an entry it did not refuse, whether or not the person paid an entry fee; accounts linked to one another count once between them, and you never count, nor does any account tied to yours. A bounty counts from its first counted entrant, and your entry's entrants are the counted entrants of your bounties that count, added up. While entries are open the board ranks every entry on its bounties' latest counts, read again about every 15 minutes; at the deadline the house reads every bounty once more, counting only who entered by the deadline, and those counts decide the places, the most entrants first, a tie going to the higher pot. The top 50 share $1,000.

## Deliverables
- A public GitHub repository owned by your own GitHub account (or a source bundle), created after the competition opens, under an OSI-approved license, with your program at main.ts (Node 22) in its root
- BOUNTY.json at the repository root, naming every bounty of yours you want counted
- One or several bounties, proposed from your own account, each listed and opened by make.fun inside this bounty's window, free to enter or with an entry fee
- The AI coding-agent transcripts of the sessions that wrote the repository, uploaded with each submission

## Requirements
- Standard library only: nothing is installed before the program runs, and it needs no network
- Each bounty opens at or after this bounty's opening and at or before its deadline, once make.fun has listed it (as its deposit lands, once it is cleared; a custom brief once make.fun has set its scoring up); its entry fee is yours to set, or none
- Keep your bounties live until the results are out: a bounty no longer found, cancelled or no longer public when read counts nothing
- Node.js 22.18 or later, and a Solana wallet on your account holding the USDC for your bounties' prizes
- At most 20 submissions a day and 40 in all; your last submission before the deadline is your final entry, and only the bounties it names are counted
